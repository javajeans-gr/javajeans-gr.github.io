Trust & privacy

Your information, your choice

Privacy has two parts. One is who in your family sees what, which you control. The other is how Ragaven stores and handles your information. This page explains both in plain words.

What Aurora Care collects

Only what the app needs to do its job for you:

  • Your name, phone number, and email, to set up your account and sign you in
  • Results of your 30-second checks
  • Medicines, appointments, lab results, and notes that you add or import
  • Your care circle: who you invited and what you allowed each person to see
  • A record of your sharing choices, so there's a clear history

What we don't do

Sharing with your care circle

Your care circle is the family members and caregivers you invite. Nobody can join without your invitation.

For each person you choose what they can see: vitals and trends, medicines, labs, appointments, and alerts. Labs are only shared if you turn them on.

A family member who makes a visit report gets only the parts you allow them to see.

Changing or stopping sharing

Open your care circle in the app to change what someone sees or to remove them. The change takes effect right away for anything new.

Removing someone stops future sharing. It can't take back what they already saw or a report you already sent them.

Camera, microphone, and location

Your iPhone asks your permission before Aurora Care can use any of these, and you can turn each one off in your iPhone settings.

Camera

Used for the 30-second check and the face check that confirms a reading is yours. The camera is only on while you're doing a check.

Microphone

Your help phrase is set up in your iPhone's own voice settings, and your iPhone listens for it. Aurora Care doesn't record your conversations.

What Aurora Care is not

Aurora Care helps you and your family notice changes and follow up sooner. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it doesn't replace your doctor. It is not a 911 or call-center service: help alerts go to the people in your care circle. If you need medical help right away, call 911.
