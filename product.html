The 30-second check

Look at your phone's front camera for about 30 seconds. Aurora Care reads your vitals from the camera and shows them next to your own usual. Heart rate and respiratory rate are measured with FDA-approved capabilities.

What it does
Captures vitals from the camera and saves them with the date and time. Measurements include heart rate and respiratory rate, both FDA-approved capabilities. Other vitals at launch: CONFIRM LIST
What it needs
An iPhone 13 or newer, good light, and your face inside the oval on screen. No watch or cuff.
Who sees it
You, and anyone in your care circle you've allowed to see vitals.
What happens next
Readings are compared with your usual. If one is outside the range you or your doctor set, your care circle can get an update.
Limits
It is not a diagnosis. Movement and poor light can affect a reading. ACCURACY WORDING · CLAIM PENDING LEGAL WORDING

Compared with your own usual

Everyone's normal is different. Aurora Care learns yours from your own readings, so a change stands out against your history, not someone else's average.

What it does
Shows each reading as in your usual range, a little outside it, or well outside it, with trends over time.
What it needs
A few days of checks to learn your usual. Until then, it uses standard ranges.
Who sees it
You, and the people you allow to see trends.
What happens next
You and your family can talk about a trend early and decide whether to call the doctor.
Limits
A trend is a reason to talk or check in. It doesn't say what is causing a change.

Your care circle, your choices

Your care circle is the family members and caregivers you invite. You decide who joins and what each person can see.

What it does
Lets you invite people and pick, for each person, what they see: vitals and trends, medicines, labs, appointments, and alerts.
What it needs
Each person's phone number or email. They accept your invite in their own Aurora Care app.
Who sees it
Only the people you invite, and only the parts you allow.
What happens next
You can change what someone sees, or remove them, at any time. Every choice is recorded so there's a clear history of who agreed to share what.
Limits
Once someone has seen information, removing them stops future sharing. It can't erase what they already saw.

Updates and alerts for your care circle

When something changes from your usual, the people you chose hear about it, so nobody has to call every day to ask.

What it does
Sends an update when a reading is outside the range set for you, a medicine is missed, or a doctor visit is missed.
What it needs
Care circle members with notifications turned on in their Aurora Care app.
Who gets it
Care circle members you've allowed to get alerts. You can set different ranges for each vital.
What happens next
They see what changed and when, and decide whether to call, visit, or suggest a doctor's visit.
Limits
Alerts depend on phones, networks, and notification settings, so delivery can be delayed. CONFIRM RE-ALERT BEHAVIOR AT LAUNCH

Voice help

Say "Hey Siri," and when Siri answers, say "Aurora help." Or set up "Aurora help" once and just say it. Either way, the people in your care circle get an alert right away, even if you can't get to your phone.

What it does
Sends an immediate-help alert to everyone in your care circle at the same time. CONFIRM: LOCATION INCLUDED
What it needs
Your phone within earshot. "Hey Siri, Aurora help" works with Siri turned on. To say "Aurora help" on its own, set it up once in your iPhone settings; Aurora Care walks you through it and lets you practice.
Who gets it
Your care circle. No one at Ragaven, and no call center, gets these alerts.
What happens next
The alert stays on their screen until someone responds. They call you, come over, or call 911.
Limits
It won't work if your phone can't hear you, is off, or has no connection. Aurora Care does not call 911. If you need medical help right away, call 911. CONFIRM: WORKS WHEN PHONE IS LOCKED

Medicines and reminders

Keep your medicine list in one place and get a reminder when it's time.

What it does
Reminds you at the times you choose and lets you mark each dose as taken.
What it needs
Your medicine list and times, entered once by you or with a family member's help.
Who sees it
You, and the people you allow to see medicines.
What happens next
Missed doses show up as a pattern, which is easier to raise with your doctor than a guess. If you allow it, your care circle gets a missed-dose alert the same day.
Limits
Aurora Care doesn't change doses or give medical advice about your medicines.

Labs and health records

Bring lab results and health records into the same place as your vitals and medicines.

What it does
Imports lab results and records from your providers through Apple Health, or lets you type them in by hand.
What it needs
Health records set up in the Apple Health app for import, or a copy of your results to enter by hand.
Who sees it
You, and the people you allow to see labs. Labs are only shared if you turn that on.
What happens next
Recent labs appear in your visit report next to vitals and medicines.
Limits
Aurora Care shows your results. It doesn't interpret them; your doctor does.

Doctor and lab appointments

Keep upcoming visits in the app with the details you need on the day.

What it does
Saves the date, time, doctor, and location for each visit and reminds you ahead of time.
What it needs
The appointment details, added by you or by a family member you allow.
Who sees it
You, and the people you allow to see appointments.
What happens next
If a visit is missed, your care circle can be told so someone can help rebook.
Limits
Aurora Care doesn't book with your doctor's office for you.

A report for doctor and lab visits

Bring one clear summary instead of trying to remember everything in the exam room.

What it does
Creates a PDF with recent vitals and trends, medicines taken and missed, recent labs, and recent alerts, each with the date it was last updated.
What it needs
Nothing extra. Tap Share Report in the app.
Who sees it
Whoever you send it to by text, email, or AirDrop. A family member can create one only with the parts you allow them to see.
What happens next
Your doctor sees the pattern, not one reading on one day.
Limits
Once shared, the PDF is a copy outside Aurora Care. CONFIRM AVAILABLE AT LAUNCH

Daily mood check-in

A one-tap way to say how you're feeling today, right from your Home Screen.

What it does
Records how you feel each day so it sits alongside your vitals.
What it needs
The Aurora Care widget on your iPhone Home Screen.
Who sees it
You, and the people you allow to see it.
What happens next
A run of hard days is easier to notice and talk about.
Limits
It is a note for you and your family, not a mental health assessment. CONFIRM AVAILABLE AT LAUNCH

Simple sign-in and a face check

No password to remember, and a quick check that each reading is really yours.

What it does
Signs you in with a code sent to your phone. Before each check, it confirms the face in the camera is yours.
What it needs
Your phone number, and a one-time face setup when you first start.
Who sees it
Your face setup stays with your account for this check only and isn't shared with your care circle.
What happens next
If someone else is in front of the camera, the reading isn't saved as yours.
