One shared picture for you and the family

When the person you care for invites you into their care circle, you see the same up-to-date information as their family. Fewer phone trees, cleaner handoffs.

How Aurora Care helps on a shift

Start with what changed

See recent vitals against the person's own usual, medicines taken or missed, and upcoming visits before you arrive.

Hand off without the phone tag

The family sees the same picture you do, so the end-of-day update is already there.

Bring one report to the doctor

Share a summary of vitals, medicines, and recent labs before a doctor or lab visit, with only the parts the person allows.

Access is the person's choice

You join a care circle by invitation from the person you care for. They decide what you see, such as vitals, medicines, appointments, or alerts. They can change your access or remove it at any time, including when your work with them ends.

Aurora Care helps you notice changes and follow up. It doesn't replace your clinical judgment, your agency's procedures, or 911.

What you'll need

  • An iPhone with the Aurora Care app
  • An invite from the person you care for
  • Notifications turned on if you've been asked to get alerts
