Personas

Two personas. One circle of care.

Aurora has two roles. The Star is the senior adult being supported. Angels are the trusted people who observe, receive alerts, and help when care action is needed.

For Stars For Angels

Star

The person at the center of care.

The Star stays independent while deciding who can see updates, receive alerts, and help with care follow-through.

Controls trust and permissions Shares vitals, labs, meds, and visits Gets support without losing dignity

Angel

The trusted circle around the Star.

Angels can be family members, nurses, doctors, or professional caregivers who help observe changes and act when needed.

Observes daily updates and trends Receives alerts for care action Helps with meds, labs, visits, and follow-up

How the relationship works

Care becomes visible without becoming intrusive.

Aurora turns ordinary check-ins into a shared care picture so families and caregivers can see what changed, what matters, and what to do next.

◉

Observe

Stars share daily signals from a mobile device, connected devices, labs, medications, appointments, and notes.

⌁

Understand

Aurora Intelligence Engine watches baseline changes and risk severity across the Star’s personal context.

!

Act

Angels get useful updates and alerts so the care circle can follow up before concern becomes crisis.

Real situations

Same roles. Many care settings.

Aging at home

Daily signals help Angels notice missed medication, weight changes, abnormal vitals, or a missed follow-up.

Care facilities

Professional caregivers and family members stay aligned around updates, alerts, and care follow-through.

When help is needed

Falls, voice-triggered help, and safe-zone breaches notify trusted Angels when support is needed.
