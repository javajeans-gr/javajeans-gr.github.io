Aurora Care Privacy Policy

Ragaven LLC, d/b/a RagavenCare and AuroraCare

Effective December 2026

Ragaven LLC, an Oregon limited liability company doing business as RagavenCare and AuroraCare ("Ragaven," "Aurora Care," "we," "us," or "our"), provides the Aurora Care mobile application, the Aurora Care web pages for Stars and Angels, and related websites and services (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It is part of our Terms of Service and our End User License Agreement (EULA). Our Consumer Health Data Policy adds detail for consumer health data; if the two conflict on consumer health data, that policy controls.

Aurora Care is a general wellness application. It is not a medical device, is not cleared or approved by the FDA, does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any disease, and is not an emergency service. You must be at least 21 years old to use it.

Key Points

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use health, biometric, or location information for advertising or profiling. Camera images and video are not saved and never reach our servers. Your face code stays on your iPhone. An Angel sees only what the Star shares, and the Star can change that at any time. Location records are deleted after 24 hours. You can download your data and delete your account in Settings.

1. Who This Policy Covers

A Star is the person whose wellness readings the Services record, usually an older adult who takes readings with their own iPhone. An Angel is a family member, friend, or caregiver a Star invites and links to the Star's account. This policy applies to both. Where a section applies to only one, it says so. Earlier versions of Aurora Care called Angels "Guardians."

2. HIPAA and How This Policy Applies

When you use Aurora Care directly, as a consumer, the information you provide is generally not "protected health information" (PHI) under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and HIPAA does not govern it. It is still health information, and we protect it as described here and in our Consumer Health Data Policy. If a healthcare provider, health plan, or other HIPAA-regulated organization provides Aurora Care to you, information we handle for that organization may be PHI. In that case we act as its business associate, and our business associate agreement with that organization and its Notice of Privacy Practices govern that information where they differ from this policy.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only what the features you use need. Many categories below — not only health and biometric information — are sensitive or regulated, including precise location, readings, medications, lab values, and account identifiers. The list below summarizes each category; the sections that follow add detail.

  • Account and contact information — name, mobile phone number, email address, date of birth or age, and sex at birth. Leaves your device: yes. Visible to your Angel: name and contact details needed for the link.
  • Camera readings (Stars) — wellness estimates (such as pulse, breathing rate, heart-rate variability, stress indicators, and blood-pressure and blood-oxygen estimates), signal-quality scores, and the date and time. Leaves your device: yes (the estimates, not images). Visible to your Angel: yes, if the Star shares readings.
  • Face template (Stars) — a mathematical representation of your face used for face verification. Leaves your device: no. Visible to your Angel: never.
  • Profile and wellness context — height, weight, smoking status, whether you take blood-pressure medication, diabetes status, goals, and alert ranges. Leaves your device: yes. Visible to your Angel: alert ranges; other items only as the App shows.
  • Apple Health data — data you choose to share from Apple Health (Section 3.4). Leaves your device: only the items listed in Section 3.4. Visible to your Angel: as the Star shares.
  • Lab results (Stars) — lab values you enter or import, such as cholesterol, and risk estimates calculated from them. Leaves your device: only after the Star's separate lab-sharing consent. Visible to your Angel: yes, if shared and the Angel's lab permission is on.
  • Medications and appointments — names, doses, schedules, reminder times, and appointment details entered by the Star or the Angel. Leaves your device: yes. Visible to your Angel: yes.
  • Precise location (Stars) — device location and the home address and Safe Zone you set. Leaves your device: yes, when Safe Zone is on. Visible to your Angel: yes, if the Angel's location permission is on (Section 3.5).
  • Safety events (Stars) — Aurora Help requests, fall and home-sensor events, "no movement" signals, and alert history and acknowledgements. Leaves your device: yes. Visible to your Angel: yes.
  • Care-link information — who is linked to whom, invitation details (including the name and phone number of a person invited), sharing permissions, and Angel actions. Leaves your device: yes.
  • Consent and audit records — the documents and consents you accepted or withdrew, with version and time, and a security log of sensitive actions. Leaves your device: yes.
  • Device and usage information — device model, operating-system and app version, push-notification token, App Attest results, IP address, time zone, sign-in code records, and, if "Help improve Aurora" is on, app usage records (Section 3.7). Leaves your device: yes.
  • Purchases — plan status, reading allowance and purchases, and transaction identifiers from Apple. We do not receive your card number. Leaves your device: yes.
  • Communications — messages you send us and records of the texts and emails we send you.

3.1 Camera readings. A reading uses the front camera for about 20 to 30 seconds. The App derives an encrypted facial blood-flow signal on your iPhone and sends that signal — not images or video — to the third-party technology provider that calculates the estimates for us (the "Third Party"), which returns them. The Third Party also receives the values it needs to calculate certain estimates: age, sex at birth, height, weight, smoking status, blood-pressure medication, and diabetes status, and technical data such as a licence token and app version. The data the App sends may also include facial landmarks — measurements of where points on your face are located — which are numbers, not images. The Third Party deletes the blood-flow signal promptly after calculating the readings. It stores the readings it calculates for Aurora Care in the United States, as Section 9 describes. Before your first camera reading, the App asks for your consent on its own screen, which explains that the Third Party receives the blood-flow signal (and any facial landmarks) and stores the readings; we keep a record of each consent, with its version and the date and time. Our servers receive the estimates, quality scores, and reading details — never camera frames, images, or video.

Your scan makes a blood-flow signal. Photos and video are never sent. The signal is sent encrypted. Our provider calculates your readings. That is its only use. It keeps results in the U.S.

3.2 Face template. Section 5 describes it in full.

3.3 Sign-in. You sign in with a one-time code sent by text message to your phone number. We keep a record of when a code was sent and whether it was accepted, with only the last four digits of your number, for 90 days, to prevent misuse and to help when sign-in fails.

3.4 Apple Health. If you grant permission, the App reads data you choose from Apple Health, which may include heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, blood pressure, steps, sleep, cardio fitness (VO₂ max), body and wrist temperature, walking steadiness, irregular-rhythm notifications, height, weight, date of birth, sex, medications, and, if you choose, clinical health records (such as lab results, conditions, medications, immunizations, allergies, procedures, and vital signs). Most of it is used only on your iPhone. These items leave your device: steps and wearable heart rate, which are stored with your readings; clinical records and lab results you choose to share; Apple Health medications, each only after you confirm it; and the demographic values sent to the Third Party (Section 3.1). Clinical notes, scanned images, raw text-recognition output, and raw health-record files are never uploaded. We never use Apple Health data for advertising, never sell it, and never disclose it to third parties for marketing or data brokering.

3.5 Location and the Safe Zone (Stars). Location is optional. If you turn on Safe Zone, give the App "Always" location permission, and agree on the location screen, the App uses iOS location services to detect when you leave or return to the Safe Zone you set around your home address, and records your location when it changes meaningfully and when your Angel asks to see where you are. Your Angel receives an alert if you leave the Safe Zone. If the Star allows an Angel to view location (the "location" permission for that Angel), the Angel can also see the Star's current location and address and recent location points, including points inside the Safe Zone. You can turn this permission off for any Angel, turn Safe Zone off, or remove location permission in iOS Settings. Our staff do not monitor your location.

3.6 Aurora Help and voice (Stars). Aurora Help is started with a Siri phrase or shortcut you set up. Aurora Care receives the request, not a recording of your voice. When the App asks you to answer "yes" out loud, Apple's speech recognition turns your answer into text, on your iPhone where possible; if that fails, Apple's servers may be used. The App does not record or keep audio.

3.7 Help improve Aurora. If this switch is on, the App records how it is used — which screens you open, what you tap, how long screens and actions take, and when the App crashes or freezes — linked to your account. These records never contain readings, lab values, medication details, location, or anything you type. We keep them for 90 days and use them only to find and fix problems and improve reliability. In the United States the switch is on unless you turn it off; elsewhere it is off unless you turn it on. Stars find it in Settings › Security and privacy; Angels in Profile › Privacy & Security.

4. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide the Services — create and secure accounts, take and show readings, compute trends and baselines, send reminders, and deliver the alerts, summaries, and reports you ask for.
  • Support the care link — share information with the Angel a Star links, as the Star's sharing settings allow.
  • Keep the Services safe and working — sign users in, confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the App, prevent fraud and abuse, investigate security incidents, and fix problems.
  • Improve reliability and features — using the usage records described in Section 3.7 and information that is aggregated or de-identified. We do not use identifiable health, biometric, Apple Health, or location information to train models, for research, or to develop unrelated products, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing profiles, or data brokering.
  • Communicate with you — service, security, plan, and administrative messages. We do not send marketing messages without your consent.
  • Meet legal obligations — comply with law and legal process, enforce our agreements, and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing. Trend indicators and range notifications are wellness prompts, not clinical assessments.

5. Your Face Template (Biometric Information)

5.1 What it is and why. With your separate consent, given on its own screen before enrollment, the App creates a face template: a mathematical representation derived from images of your face, used only to confirm that the enrolled Star is the person taking a reading, so readings are attributed to the right person. It is not a photograph or video and is designed so that your facial image cannot be reconstructed from it. It is never used to identify you to anyone else or to match you against any other database.

5.2 Where it is kept. The face template is stored encrypted on your iPhone, protected by the iOS Keychain and your device's Face ID or passcode. It is not uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone, including your Angel. It is never sold, leased, traded, or otherwise disclosed for value. If we later offer to store a copy in our cloud so face verification works on a new phone, we will do so only if you separately turn that on after we explain its purpose and retention.

5.3 Help from others. A Star may ask an Angel or family member for help understanding this choice, but only the Star can give or withdraw biometric consent, unless the Star cannot make this decision and a person with legal authority to act for the Star (such as an agent under a health-care power of attorney or a court-appointed guardian) does so, as Section 1.3 of the Terms of Service describes.

5.4 Retention and destruction schedule. We keep a face template until the earliest of: (a) you withdraw your biometric consent or delete your face template in Settings; (b) your account is deleted; or (c) three (3) years after your last interaction with Aurora Care. We then permanently destroy it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. This schedule is published to meet the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act and similar laws.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows.

6.1 With your Angel. A Star's readings, alerts, medications, appointments, and — if the Star allows — lab results and location are visible only to Angels the Star has linked, and only as the Star's sharing settings allow. Face templates are never shared. A Star can change sharing or remove an Angel at any time.

6.2 With service providers that process information on our behalf under written contracts limiting their use of it to providing services to us, requiring security and confidentiality, and requiring them to delete it when we ask or, for the Third Party, as Section 9 describes:

  • Amazon Web Services, Inc. — hosting, databases, storage, encryption keys, sign-in (Amazon Cognito), push-notification relay, and email delivery (Amazon SES), in the United States (US East, Ohio).
  • The Third Party — camera-based wellness estimation. It receives the encrypted blood-flow signal, any facial landmarks, and the values listed in Section 3.1, deletes the signal promptly after calculating the readings, and stores the readings in the United States (Sections 3.1 and 9).
  • Twilio Inc. — text messages: sign-in codes, invitations, account and plan notices, and alert fallback. Messages do not contain health details.
  • Apple Inc. — push-notification delivery (which receives device and delivery information), App Attest, speech recognition (Section 3.6), Apple Health and HomeKit (on your device), and App Store payments.

6.3 At your direction — for example, when you export your data or share a report. Once information leaves Aurora Care, the recipient's handling of it is outside our control.

6.4 For legal reasons — when we believe in good faith that the law, a subpoena, or a court order requires it; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Ragaven, our users, or others; or to investigate fraud or security issues. Where permitted, we will try to tell you about requests for your information.

6.5 In a business transaction — such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this policy and applicable law. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy, and biometric information will be transferred only as biometric privacy law permits.

6.6 De-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify you. We will not try to re-identify it.

7. Information on an Angel's Phone

To show a Star's recent information without a network connection, the App on an Angel's phone keeps an encrypted local copy, protected by iOS file protection and the Angel's passcode. When a care link ends — because the Star removes the Angel, the Angel leaves, or the Star's account is deleted — our servers stop sending that Angel the Star's information, and the Angel's App deletes its local copy of the Star's information when it receives the notice or next connects. If the Angel's phone stays offline, or the App has been removed from that phone, the copy remains on the phone until it reconnects or is erased. Angels may not copy a Star's information outside the App and must delete any copy they made when asked by the Star.

8. Notifications and Messages

Push notifications, text messages, and emails do not contain readings, medication names, diagnoses, or other health details; they say, for example, that a Star asked for help, and the details open inside the App. Even so, a notification on a lock screen may let someone near the phone see that an alert happened. You can hide previews or turn notifications off in iOS Settings; doing so means you may miss alerts and reminders. Alerts are best-effort and may be delayed or not delivered (see the Terms). Message and data rates may apply to texts.

9. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, fulfil the purposes in this policy, comply with law, meet our obligations to any healthcare organization that provides the Services to you, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect the security of the Services. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it, unless the law requires or permits us to keep it or we need it to establish or defend legal claims. Where we handle PHI for a HIPAA-regulated organization, we keep and dispose of it as HIPAA, our business associate agreement, and that organization's instructions require. Specific periods:

  • Camera images and video — not stored.
  • Location records — deleted automatically 24 hours after they are created.
  • Face template — Section 5.4.
  • Readings, alerts (including falls and other safety events), lab values, medications and their change history, appointments, and other account data — kept while your account is open, and deleted with the account.
  • Invitation records — the name and phone number of a person invited expire after 24 hours.
  • Usage records and sign-in code records — 90 days.
  • Reading history cache on your iPhone — up to 90 days.
  • Backups — encrypted backups expire within 35 days.
  • Readings stored by the Third Party — kept while our contract with the Third Party is in effect. The Third Party deletes them within 60 days after that contract ends, and copies in its backups are removed as its backup cycle runs, which has no fixed end date.
  • Records kept after an account is deleted — consent records and security audit logs (up to seven years), billing, purchase, and reading-allowance records (up to seven years, for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes), a one-way coded form of your phone number (so that a once-per-person welcome offer is not claimed twice), a record that the deletion was carried out (about 13 months), and anything under a legal hold, until the hold ends. We restrict these records from any other use.

9.1 Deleting your account. You can delete your account in Settings. Deletion begins when you confirm it and cannot be cancelled. It removes your account and data from our databases, sign-in system, and push registrations, generally within 30 days, except for the records listed above. Readings the Third Party stores for us are deleted on the schedule listed above. Linked Angels are told the link has ended, and their App deletes its copy (Section 7).

9.2 When a plan ends. If a plan ends and is not renewed, we notify the Star and every linked Angel by push notification and email on days 1, 7, 21, and 29 after the plan ends; the last notice is also sent by text message. A 30-day grace period begins when the first notice reaches the Star. During it, readings and location sharing are paused; fall alerts, Safe Zone alerts, and Aurora Help that the Star switched on keep working; and the Star can download their data or take over the plan. If no one renews within 30 days after the first notice, we delete the Star's account and its data as in Section 9.1. While Apple is still retrying a failed payment, we wait up to 60 days after the plan ended. We delete an account for non-payment only if the Star — and, when an Angel pays, that Angel — agreed to this rule; Section 8 of the Terms explains when that happens.

10. Your Choices and Rights

10.1 Controls in the App. You can grant or remove camera, location, Apple Health, notification, Bluetooth, motion, and Face ID permissions in iOS Settings; turn Safe Zone, Aurora Help, fall alerts, lab sharing, and "Help improve Aurora" on or off; change what each Angel may see or remove an Angel; view and edit your profile, medications, and other information you entered; withdraw your Health Data Authorization; delete your face template; download your data (Settings › Download my data); and delete your account. Withdrawing a consent stops the processing it allowed from then on; it does not undo processing that already happened, and features that need that consent stop working.

10.2 Download your data. The download is an encrypted file covering your account information, readings, location records still held, medications, lab values, alerts, appointments, care links, and consent records. When it is ready we notify you in the App and give you a short-lived, secure link.

10.3 U.S. state privacy rights. Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Montana, Nevada, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia, and Washington), you may have the right to know and access the personal information we hold; correct it; delete it; get a portable copy; opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising (we do none of these); limit or withdraw consent to the use of sensitive information, including health, biometric, and precise location information; appeal our decision; and not be discriminated against for using these rights. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where required.

10.4 California. In the past 12 months we collected the categories in Section 3 (identifiers; personal records; characteristics such as age and sex; commercial information; biometric information; internet or device activity; geolocation; sensory information, meaning camera input processed in real time; and sensitive personal information, including health, biometric, and precise location information) from you and your device, for the purposes in Section 4, and disclosed them to the recipients in Section 6. We do not sell or share personal information and use sensitive personal information only for purposes California law permits.

10.5 Consumer health data laws. Our Consumer Health Data Policy, available in the App and at our website, describes our practices and your rights under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, and similar laws.

10.6 How to make a request. Use the controls in Settings, or email contact@ragavencare.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity first, which may include a code sent to the phone on your account. An authorized agent may act for you where the law allows; we may ask for proof of authority. We will respond within the time the law requires (generally 45 days, extendable where permitted). If we decline, you may appeal by replying with the subject "Privacy Appeal," and if you are not satisfied with the result, you may contact your state attorney general.

11. Where the Services Are Offered

Aurora Care is offered to users in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. We do not offer the Services to people in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or other places whose laws restrict transfers of personal information to the United States.

12. Children

Aurora Care is only for adults aged 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Information About Others

If you are an Angel, you see a Star's information only because the Star chose to share it. Use it only to support that Star's wellness and safety, keep it confidential, and do not copy, disclose, or use it for anything else. If you enter information about someone else (for example, an appointment for a Star or an invitation to a new Angel), you confirm you have their permission.

14. How We Protect Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted storage on your iPhone, access controls that limit each account's information to its owner and linked Angels, least-privilege access for our staff, security logging, and checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone, passcode, and sign-in codes secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires, including under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, state breach-notification laws, and, where applicable, the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule.

15. Third-Party Services

Your use of Apple services is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. The Third Party's processing of your information for us is described in Sections 3.1, 6.2, and 9. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated policy in the App and on our website with a new "Last Updated" date and keep prior versions. For a material change — especially to how we use or share health, biometric, or location information — we will notify you in the App or by email before it applies to you, and where the law requires consent, the change will not apply to you until you give it. Continued use alone is not consent to new uses of sensitive information. Information is handled under the version in effect when it was collected unless you agree otherwise.
