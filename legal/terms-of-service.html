2. What Aurora Care Is

Aurora Care is a general wellness application. It helps Stars and the Angels they choose observe general wellness patterns over time, notice changes from a Star's own usual readings, keep track of medications and appointments, and stay connected. It is designed to help a Star decide whether to seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional. It does not make that decision, and it does not provide medical judgment.

The Services may include camera-based wellness estimates; trends and personal baselines; notifications when a reading falls outside a range; Apple Health integration; lab-value tracking and general risk-education estimates; medication and appointment reminders; Safe Zone location notifications; Aurora Help (voice help) requests; fall and home-sensor events; and alerts to Angels. Features vary by version and plan and may change.
