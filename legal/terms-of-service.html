2. What Aurora Care Is

Aurora Care is a general wellness application. It helps people ("Stars") and the family members, friends, and caregivers they choose ("Angels") observe general wellness patterns over time, notice changes from a Star's own usual readings, keep track of medications and appointments, and stay connected. It is designed to help you decide whether to seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional — it does not make that decision for you, and it does not provide medical judgment.

The Services may include: camera-based wellness estimates; trends and personal baselines; threshold notifications you configure; Apple Health integration; lab-value tracking and general risk-education estimates; medication and appointment reminders; Safe Zone location notifications; Voice Help requests; HomeKit sensor events; and alerts to Angels. Features vary by version and may change.
