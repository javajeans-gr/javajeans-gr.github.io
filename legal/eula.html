6. Face Verification and Biometric Information

6.1 What is created and why. If you are a Star, the Licensed Application asks for your separate consent, on its own screen, before it creates a face template. A face template is a mathematical representation derived from images of your face captured by the front camera, created solely to confirm that the enrolled Star is the person taking a reading, so that readings are attributed to the right person. It is not a photograph or video and is designed so that your facial image cannot be reconstructed from it. Camera images are processed in real time and are not stored. Accepting this EULA is not consent to create a face template.

6.2 Where it is kept. The face template is stored encrypted on your iPhone, protected by the iOS Keychain and your device's Face ID or passcode. It is not uploaded to Ragaven's servers or to anyone else. If Ragaven later offers to keep a copy in its cloud so that face verification works on a new phone, that copy will be stored only if you separately turn it on, after we tell you its purpose and retention.

6.3 No sale or disclosure. Ragaven will not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric information, will not share your face template with Angels, will not use it to identify you to anyone or to match you against any other database, and will not disclose it except with your consent or as required by law.

6.4 Camera signal processing by a third-party provider. To calculate readings, the Licensed Application derives an encrypted facial blood-flow signal on your device and sends that signal — not images or video — to the third-party technology provider that calculates the readings (the "Third Party"), as the Privacy Policy describes. The data the Licensed Application sends may also include facial landmarks — measurements of where points on your face are located — which are numbers, not images. The Third Party deletes the blood-flow signal promptly after calculating the readings and stores the readings in the United States. Before your first camera reading, the Licensed Application asks for your consent to this on its own screen, and Ragaven keeps a record of that consent.

6.5 Withdrawal, retention, and destruction. You may withdraw your biometric consent and delete your face template at any time in Settings; camera readings then stop until you enroll again. Biometric information is retained and permanently destroyed under the schedule in Section 5 of the Privacy Policy.
