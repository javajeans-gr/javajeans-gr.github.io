5. Biometric Data and Consent

5.1 What is created. With your permission, the Licensed Application creates a one-way mathematical face template from the front-camera image solely to (a) verify that the enrolled user is the person taking a reading and (b) recognize the enrolled user on that user's own devices. The face template is not a photograph or video, and the facial image cannot be reconstructed from it. Camera images are processed in real time and are not stored by the Licensed Application.

5.2 On-device by default. The face template is stored encrypted on your device.

5.3 Separate, optional cloud storage. An encrypted copy of the face template is stored in Ragaven's cloud only if you give separate, affirmative, written (electronic) consent through a dedicated in-app consent screen. Accepting this EULA does not by itself enable cloud storage of your face template. The option is off by default, and declining it does not limit any other feature.

5.4 Camera-signal processing by NuraLogix. When the NuraLogix (DeepAffex / Anura) engine is used, an encrypted facial blood-flow signal derived on your device — not images or video — is sent to NuraLogix Corporation to calculate wellness estimates, as described in the Privacy Policy.

5.5 No sale or disclosure. Ragaven will not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric data, will not share your face template with Angels, and will not disclose it to anyone except as required by law or with your consent.

5.6 Retention and destruction. Biometric data is retained and permanently destroyed according to the schedule published in Section 8 of the Privacy Policy: at the earliest of withdrawal of consent, account deletion, or three (3) years after your last interaction with Aurora Care. You may withdraw consent and delete your face template at any time in Settings.
