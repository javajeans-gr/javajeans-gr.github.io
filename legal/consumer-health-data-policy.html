Aurora Care Consumer Health Data Policy

Ragaven LLC, d/b/a RagavenCare and AuroraCare

Effective December 2026

Ragaven LLC, doing business as RagavenCare and AuroraCare ("Ragaven," "Aurora Care," "we," "us," or "our"), provides the Aurora Care mobile application, the Aurora Care web pages for Stars and Angels, and related services (together, the "Services"). This policy explains how we handle consumer health data under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370, the consumer health data provisions of the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, and similar laws. It supplements our Privacy Policy; if the two conflict on consumer health data, this policy controls.

Aurora Care is a general wellness application. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any disease, and is not an emergency service. When you use it directly, your information is generally not "protected health information" under HIPAA.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy covers consumers in Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut, and anyone whose consumer health data we collect or process in those states. It applies to Stars (the people whose wellness readings the Services record) and Angels (the family members, friends, or caregivers a Star links with). You must be at least 21 years old to use Aurora Care.

2. What Consumer Health Data Is

Consumer health data is personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status, including information we derive or infer, such as a trend or a risk estimate. Some information that is not health information by itself — such as precise location, or the fact that you use Aurora Care — may reveal health status in context, and we treat it with the same care.

3. Consumer Health Data We Collect

Depending on the features you use:

  • Camera readings (Stars): estimates such as pulse, breathing rate, heart-rate variability, stress indicators, and blood-pressure and blood-oxygen estimates, with quality scores and the date and time. Camera images and video are not stored and never reach our servers.
  • Biometric face template (Stars): a mathematical representation of your face, used only to confirm that the enrolled Star is the person taking a reading. It is kept encrypted on your iPhone and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Health context: age or date of birth, sex at birth, height, weight, smoking status, blood-pressure medication, diabetes status, wellness goals, and alert ranges.
  • Apple Health data you choose to share, as described in Section 3.4 of the Privacy Policy, including clinical health records if you choose.
  • Lab results (Stars): values you enter or import, such as cholesterol, and risk estimates calculated from them.
  • Medications and appointments.
  • Precise location (Stars): if you turn on Safe Zone, your location and the home address and Safe Zone you set.
  • Safety events (Stars): Aurora Help requests, fall and home-sensor events, "no movement" signals, and alert history.
  • Trends and inferences: personal baselines, trends, and changes against your own history and ranges.

4. Where It Comes From

  • You, when you enter information or take a reading.
  • Your device: the camera, motion and Bluetooth signals for features you turn on, and location if you allow it.
  • Your linked Angel, for example when an Angel enters an appointment or proposes a medication change (which takes effect only after the Star confirms it) or adjusts an alert range (which the Star is told about and can undo).
  • Apple Health and HomeKit, with your permission.
  • The third-party technology provider that calculates camera readings for us (the "Third Party"), which returns the estimates calculated from the encrypted facial blood-flow signal your device sends it.

5. Why We Collect and Use It

Only to provide the Services you ask for (readings, trends, reminders, alerts, summaries, and reports); to share it with the Angel a Star links, as the Star's settings allow; to keep the Services safe and working; to improve reliability using aggregated or de-identified information; and to meet legal obligations. We never use consumer health data for advertising, marketing profiles, model training, or research.

6. Your Consent

To collect. We collect consumer health data only with your consent or as necessary to provide a Service you ask for. A Star gives a Health Data Authorization when creating an account, and the App asks separately before the first camera reading (explaining that the Third Party receives the encrypted blood-flow signal and any facial landmarks, and stores the readings), before it creates a face template, before it uses location for Safe Zone, and before lab results leave the Star's iPhone. Camera, location, Apple Health, notification, Bluetooth, motion, and Face ID access also each need your permission in iOS.

To share. We share consumer health data only with your consent or as necessary to provide a Service you ask for. A Star's information reaches an Angel only after the Star links that Angel, and the Star chooses, for each Angel, whether the Angel may see lab results and location.

To withdraw. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings (including withdrawing your Health Data Authorization, deleting your face template, turning off lab sharing or Safe Zone, or changing an Angel's permissions), in iOS Settings, or by emailing us. Withdrawal stops the processing from then on, and features that need that consent stop working.

7. Who We Share It With

We do not sell consumer health data. We share it only:

  • With the Star's linked Angel, as the Star's settings allow. Face templates are never shared.
  • With service providers that process it for us under written contracts that limit their use, require security and confidentiality, and require deletion when we pass on a deletion request (for the Third Party, as Section 9 describes): Amazon Web Services, Inc. (hosting, storage, sign-in, notifications, and email, in the United States); the Third Party (camera-based wellness estimation; it receives the encrypted blood-flow signal, any facial landmarks (measurements of where points on the face are located), and the health context values needed for certain estimates, not images or video, deletes the signal promptly after calculating the readings, and stores the readings in the United States); Twilio Inc. (text messages, which contain no health details); and Apple Inc. (push notifications, speech recognition, Apple Health and HomeKit on your device, and App Store payments).
  • At your direction, for example when you download your data or share a report.
  • For legal reasons, when we believe in good faith that the law, a subpoena, or a court order requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.
  • In a business transaction, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy and applicable law.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

8. Geofencing

We do not set up a geofence around any place that provides in-person health care services, and we do not use location to identify or track people who visit such a place or to send them messages or ads. The only geofence in Aurora Care is the Safe Zone a Star sets around their own home address, used only to tell whether the Star has left it.

9. How Long We Keep It

  • Camera images and video: not stored.
  • Location records: deleted automatically 24 hours after they are created.
  • Face template: until you withdraw biometric consent or delete it, your account is deleted, or three years after your last interaction with Aurora Care, whichever is first; then destroyed within 30 days.
  • Readings, alerts (including fall and safety events), lab values, medications, appointments, and other account data: kept while your account is open and deleted with the account.
  • Encrypted backups: expire within 35 days.
  • Readings stored by the Third Party: kept while our contract with the Third Party is in effect and deleted by the Third Party within 60 days after that contract ends; copies in its backups are removed as its backup cycle runs, which has no fixed end date.
  • After deletion we keep only consent and security audit records, billing and purchase records, a record that the deletion was carried out, and data under a legal hold, as Section 9 of the Privacy Policy describes, and we restrict them from any other use.

An account is deleted when you ask us to delete it, or when a plan ends and is not renewed within the grace period described in Section 9.2 of the Privacy Policy and Section 8 of the Terms of Service.

10. Your Rights

Subject to the law where you live, you have the right to:

  • Confirm and access whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it.
  • Know who received it: a list of the third parties and affiliates with whom we shared it, and how to contact them.
  • Delete it. When we delete it, we also tell our service providers to delete it. Readings the Third Party stores for us are deleted on the schedule in Section 9.
  • Withdraw consent to our collection or sharing of it.
  • Appeal if we decline your request.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11. How to Exercise Your Rights

Use the controls in Settings, including Settings › Download my data and account deletion, or email contact@ragavencare.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity first, which may include a code sent to the phone on your account. An authorized agent may act for you where the law allows; we may ask for proof of authority. We will respond within the time the law requires, generally 45 days, and will tell you if we need to extend it. If we decline, you may appeal by replying with the subject "Privacy Appeal." If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may contact your state attorney general.

12. No Sale of Consumer Health Data

We do not sell consumer health data, do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not show advertising in Aurora Care.

13. How We Protect It

We use encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted storage on your iPhone, access controls that limit each account's information to its owner and linked Angels, and security logging. Only staff who need consumer health data to provide the Services may access it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated policy in the App and on our website with a new "Last Updated" date. Before a material change to how we collect, use, or share consumer health data applies to you, we will notify you and, where the law requires, obtain your consent.
