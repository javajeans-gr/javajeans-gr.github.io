Do you really know how your parent is doing beyond a casual check-in?

Aurora helps families observe health warning signs early and keep care connected to action.

The Star shares signals from the mobile device they already use, regularly: camera-captured FDA approved heart rate and respiratory rate, blood pressure checks, labs, medications, appointments, trends, and notes.

Trusted Angels, including family members and professional caregivers, can observe updates, receive alerts, and help when needed.

Aurora Intelligence turns scattered signals into care intelligence for proactive action: heart risk, stroke risk, UTI risk, pneumonia risk, falls, delirium, and more.

Aurora is built for connected care across families, communities, and facilities.

The result: independence for Stars. Informed care actions for Angels.
