# Aurora Bithuman Video Briefs

Use the pitch-deck title slide language exactly:

**Aurora by Ragaven**  
**Keeping Senior Adults Independent, and Their Families Informed.**

Do not use: "Care intelligence for aging adults."

## Video 1: Aurora Overview

Target length: 45-55 seconds

Title card:
Aurora by Ragaven  
Keeping Senior Adults Independent, and Their Families Informed.

Voiceover:
Do you really know how your parent is doing beyond a casual check-in?

Aurora helps families observe senior health warning signs early and keep care connected to take actions.

The Star shares daily signals from the mobile device they already use: vitals, labs, medications, appointments, trends, and notes.

Trusted Angels, including family members and professional caregivers, can observe updates, receive alerts, and help when needed.

Aurora Intelligence Engine turns scattered signals into care intelligence for proactive action: heart risk, stroke risk, UTI risk, pneumonia risk, falls, delirium, and more.

Aurora is built for connected care for families, communities, and facilities.

Visual flow:
Title slide -> Star using mobile device -> Angels observing updates -> Aurora Intelligence Engine -> care alerts -> connected care circle.

On-screen captions:
Observe warning signs early.
Keep care connected to take actions.
Daily signals become proactive care action.

## Video 2: Star Values

Target length: 40-50 seconds

Title card:
Aurora for Stars

Voiceover:
Aurora helps the Star stay independent with dignity, privacy, and support.

The Star uses a mobile device, such as a smartphone or tablet, to capture and share the signals that matter: vitals, labs, medications, appointments, trends, and personal notes.

The Star chooses trusted Angels who can observe updates and help when needed.

Aurora Help gives the Star a simple voice-triggered way to ask for help when movement is difficult.

Aurora keeps the Star at the center of care, while making everyday check-ins more informed and useful.

Visual flow:
Star title -> mobile device capture -> vitals/labs/meds/visits icons -> trusted Angel circle -> Aurora Help -> independent senior at home.

On-screen captions:
Mobile device first.
Star-controlled sharing.
Check-ins become care action.

## Video 3: Angel Values

Target length: 40-50 seconds

Title card:
Aurora for Angels

Voiceover:
Aurora helps Angels move from, "I hope Mom is okay," to, "I know what is changing, and I know when to act."

Angels can observe daily updates, trend drifts, and care alerts from the Star.

Examples include BP above baseline, medication missed, doctor follow-up due, abnormal vitals and risks, fall detected, safe-zone breach, and Aurora Help voice triggers.

Aurora gives families and professional caregivers earlier visibility, clearer context, and daily peace of mind.

Visual flow:
Angel title -> son/daughter/professional caregiver using mobile device -> alert examples -> safe-zone breach -> care action -> peace of mind.

On-screen captions:
Observe changes sooner.
Care alerts communicated.
Help when needed.
