What Aurora Intelligence watches

Personal baseline, meaningful change, clearer follow-up.

1

Collect the signals

Vitals, labs, medications, routines, symptoms, appointments, and safe-zone events come together in one care context.

2

Compare to baseline

Aurora looks for changes against the Star's profile and history instead of treating every reading as isolated.

3

Communicate next steps

Stars and trusted Angels get clearer updates so they can decide when follow-up or care action is needed.

Aurora supports earlier care awareness and follow-up. It does not replace clinicians, emergency services, or medical diagnosis.
