What Aurora Care Intelligence watches

Personal baseline, meaningful change, clearer follow-up.

1

Collect the signals

Vitals, labs, medications, routines, symptoms, appointments, and safe-zone events come together in one care context.

2

Compare to baseline

Aurora Care looks for changes against your parent’s own profile and history instead of treating every reading as isolated.

3

Communicate next steps

Your parent and the people they chose get clearer updates, so they can decide when follow-up is needed.
