Care changes with life

The questions change across the 60s, 70s, and 80s.

Aging is personal, but the care conversation often moves from protecting independence to managing change and coordinating support.

60s

Protect independence

Are routines, medications, and health baselines staying steady?

Establish the everyday picture
70s

Manage change

Are trends drifting across vitals, labs, conditions, or appointments?

Notice change before urgency
80s

Coordinate support

Does mobility, cognition, or recovery require faster shared action?
