About Aurora Care

Getting older shouldn't mean giving up control

And loving someone who's getting older shouldn't mean living with constant worry. Too often, both sides are left guessing.

Why we built Aurora Care

Most people want to stay in their own homes as they get older. Their families want to help without hovering. Between visits and phone calls, though, nobody has a clear picture of how things are really going.

Aurora Care is built around the person at the center of care. A quick daily check on their own phone captures their vitals, alongside their medicines, labs, and appointments. Aurora Care learns what's usual for them and shows when something changes.

They decide who's in their care circle and what each person sees. The family members and caregivers they invite get a clear picture and a clear next step, so they can step in at the right moment, not every moment.

What we believe

  • The person getting care stays in charge.
  • The phone you already own should be enough.
  • Families deserve facts, not guesses.
  • Health tools should be honest about what they can and can't do.
