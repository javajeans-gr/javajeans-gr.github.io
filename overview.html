How it works

From a 30-second check to the right next step.

Each day your parent takes a 30-second check on their own phone. Aurora Care compares it with their own usual and keeps their medicines, labs, and appointments in one place, so the people they chose can act early or step in fast.

The daily routine

Measure, observe, act.

01

Measure

A 30-second check with the phone’s camera, each morning. No extra device.

02

Observe

Each reading is compared with their own usual, so a change is easier to see.

03

Act early

When something trends the wrong way, the people they chose see it while there’s still time to talk it through.

04

Get help right away

If they need help right away, they can say “Hey Siri, Aurora help,” and the people they chose get an alert.
