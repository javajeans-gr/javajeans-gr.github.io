For families

Know how Mom is really doing, between calls

Every morning you call, and every morning she says she's fine. Aurora Care shows you both what's changed, so the next call is about the right thing.

Get launch updates See everyday examples

Whether you live down the street or across the country

If you live nearby, you're probably the one who drives to appointments and picks up prescriptions. If you live far away, you often hear about a problem days later, secondhand.

Either way, you're filling in gaps with phone calls. Aurora Care gives you and your parent the same picture: vitals compared with their own usual, medicines taken or missed, upcoming visits, and recent labs.

Your parent stays in charge

Your mom or dad sets up Aurora Care on their own phone and invites you into their care circle. They decide what you see, and they can change it at any time.

It also means Aurora Care is something you set up with them, not something you set up about them.

Everyday moments

What Aurora Care helps with

Mom's blood pressure has crept up all week

What you see
Her readings against her usual, and two missed evening doses.
A good next step
Call with the trend in hand, help her book the doctor, and bring the report.

Dad keeps forgetting his evening pills

What you see
A medicine chart with the missed days.
A good next step
A calm talk about his routine, and a pattern to raise with his doctor.

Coming home after a hospital stay

What you see
Daily vitals and medicines in one view for everyone helping.
A good next step
The whole family knows how the first weeks are going without a group text.

Mom needs help and her phone is across the room

What you see
An immediate-help alert on your phone, sent when she says her help phrase. Her phone needs to be close enough to hear her.
A good next step
Call her, go over, or call 911. Aurora Care doesn't call 911 itself.

A doctor visit slips through the cracks

What you see
A note that a scheduled visit was missed.
A good next step
Help her rebook while it's still fresh.

Your brother wants to help too

What you see
The same picture he sees, if Mom invites you both.
A good next step
Stop being the family switchboard. Everyone reads the same update.

What you see, and what you don't

Here's an example of what a parent might choose. Every person in the care circle can have different settings.

She shares with you

  • Vitals and trends compared with her usual
  • Medicines taken and missed
  • Upcoming doctor and lab visits
  • Immediate-help alerts

She keeps to herself

  • Lab results, until she decides to share them
  • Her daily mood check-in
  • Anything she turns off later

Setting it up together

The easiest time is your next visit, or on a video call. It takes one sitting.

CONFIRM SETUP TIME AND HELP OPTIONS AT LAUNCH

  1. She installs Aurora Care on her iPhoneAn iPhone 13 or newer. No other device needed.
  2. She signs in with her phone numberA text code, no password to remember.
  3. She sets up her face check and does a first 30-second checkSo every reading is confirmed to be hers.
  4. She invites you and picks what you seeYou accept in your own Aurora Care app.
  5. She sets up her help phraseThe app walks her through it and lets her practice.
