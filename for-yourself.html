For older adults

Your health, your call. Your people, in the loop.

A 30-second check on your own phone shows how today compares with your usual. You decide who sees it, and what they see.

Get launch updates What a day looks like

You're in charge of your own information

Aurora Care is yours. It lives on your phone, under your name. Nobody joins your care circle unless you invite them, and nobody sees anything you haven't chosen to share.

Want your daughter to see your blood pressure but not your labs? Want your neighbor to get help alerts and nothing else? You can set that up for each person, and change it whenever you like.

Fewer "just checking in" calls

When your family can see you're doing well, they don't need to ask every day. When something changes, you can talk about it together, with the facts in front of you both.

What a day with Aurora Care looks like

Your part takes about a minute. The rest happens on its own.

Morning: your 30-second check

Open the app, look at the camera, and tap Start. You'll see today's readings next to your usual.

Through the day: reminders

A reminder when it's time for a medicine, and a tap to mark it taken. A reminder before doctor or lab visits.

Any time: help by voice

If you need someone right away, say "Hey Siri, Aurora help," or just "Aurora help" once it's set up. Your care circle gets an alert. Your phone needs to be close enough to hear you.
